2. What this is

"Clip Autopilot" is a private tool used only by the operator to publish clips to the operator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok channels via the official platform APIs. It offers no accounts or services to third parties and does not collect data of other users.

3. This website

This website uses no cookies, trackers or external scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open a page, GitHub processes your IP address and technical request data to deliver the site and keep it secure; this may involve a transfer to the USA. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure, working website).

4. Data processed by the tool

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens of the operator's own platform accounts
  • Platform account IDs of those accounts
  • IDs and URLs of published posts
  • View and engagement statistics of those posts and channels
  • Public metadata of YouTube videos considered as clip sources (title, channel name, statistics)

5. Purpose and legal basis

Purposes: publishing clips, choosing source videos, tracking performance and operating the channels. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in operating and evaluating our own channels).

6. Storage location and retention

Data of the tool is stored on a server of Hetzner Online GmbH in the EU. Tokens are kept until revoked or no longer needed; post IDs, URLs and statistics are kept as long as the related channels are active, then deleted.

7. Recipients

  • Google (YouTube Data API)
  • Meta Platforms (Instagram API)
  • TikTok (Content Posting API)
  • OpusClip (cutting source videos into clips)
  • Anthropic (text analysis of public video titles, descriptions and transcripts; no personal data is intended to be sent)
  • GitHub (hosting of this website)

These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, e.g. the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen, Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf. Write to autorun.clips@gmail.com; we will delete data on request.

9. Revoking platform access

The account owner can revoke access at any time: Instagram (Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites), TikTok (Settings → Security & permissions → Apps), Google (myaccount.google.com/permissions). After revocation, stored tokens become unusable and are deleted.

10. Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is used only to publish videos to and read statistics of the operator's own channels. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide the function, and not used to train generalized AI models. We also comply with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
